# Lips

An experimental tool that reads the speech of a person in silent video and turns it into English text, without using any audio.

## Language

**Lip reading**:
Recovering spoken words from the visible movement of a speaker's mouth and face alone.
_Avoid_: VSR (in prose), video-to-text, captioning, scene description

**Speaker**:
The single, mostly frontal person in the video whose speech is being read.
_Avoid_: Subject, face, user

**Transcript**:
The English text produced by lip reading a speaker.
_Avoid_: Caption, subtitles, description

**Live stream**:
Video arriving continuously in real time, where the transcript must keep up as the speaker talks.
_Avoid_: Feed, real-time video

**Recording**:
A finished video file, read in full before or while the transcript is produced.
_Avoid_: Clip (unless short), offline video
